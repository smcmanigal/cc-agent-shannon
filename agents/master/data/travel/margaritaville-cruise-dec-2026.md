## Excursion shortlist (researched 2026-10-01, adult prices per person)

Note: no true cave or cenote diving is offered. The closest is the cenote float below; certified scuba is open-water reef drift diving.

**Water adventure**
- Cozumel Two-Dive Certified Scuba, $159.99. Two-tank drift dives in the Arrecifes de Cozumel marine park. C-card and a dive in the last 2 years required. Gear included; wetsuit extra; no sunscreen allowed.
- Jungle Cenote at Kanan Park & Snorkel, $79.99. Walk through a small cave to a freshwater cenote (float in a tube), reef snorkel, Playa Uvas beach, tequila tasting. 4.5 hrs. Must swim.
- Coral Reef Restoration Program, $69.99. Hands-on with marine biologists, guided snorkel over coral nurseries. 3 hrs.
- Stingray, Sharks & Beach Party, $109.99. Boat, two snorkel stops, stingrays and nurse sharks, beach BBQ.
- Also: Beginner Scuba $129.99, Snuba $99.99, Sea Trek helmet dive $129.99, Clear Kayak & Snorkel $89.99.

**Outdoor / history combined**
- Jeep Adventure to Punta Sur, $119.99. Drive a Jeep (21+ with license, 4 per jeep) to El Caracol Mayan temple, climb Celarain lighthouse, reef snorkel, Explora Beach. 5 hrs, moderate. Best all-round pick.
- ATV & Beach Adventure $139.99; Buggy & Beach Snorkel $129.99.

**Historical / cultural**
- Mayan Ruins & Chocolate Tasting, $99.99. San Gervasio, the island's largest Mayan site, with INAH-certified guides, then the Mayan Cacao Company. 4 hrs.
- Best Foodies & Mayan Heritage Park, $129.99. Local food, heritage park ceremonies, chocolate and honey.
- Experience a Traditional Mexican Christmas, $99.99 (seasonal, fits a Dec sailing).

**Skipped as shopping/drinking-focused:** Best of Cozumel Tour & Shopping, Downtown Shopping & Beach Break, the beach-club and open-bar passes.
