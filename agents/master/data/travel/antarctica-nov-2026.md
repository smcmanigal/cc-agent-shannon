- **Two separate forms, one per passenger.** Both reminder emails go to shannon@mcmanigal.net, sent ~20 seconds apart, addressed "Dear Shannon McManigal" and "Dear Hannah Freeman". Shannon controls both; nothing was sent to Hannah's own address. The salutation is the only way to tell the two links apart.
- **The link is stable and reusable** — the token is identical across all four reminders (9 Jul, 12 Aug, 25 Aug, 9 Sep), so it is not single-use and has not expired. Returning to it later is safe.
- **The "PIF Manual" in the booking documents is for booking agents only** and is not Shannon's document — the booking email says so explicitly: *"PIF Manual (for booking agents only)* … *As direct client you will receive a separate email with a link to your Personal Information Form (P.I.F.) Please submit the P.I.F. online latest 3 months prior to departure."* The link is 403-blocked anyway.
